La Señal

"La señal en medio del ruido."

What it is

La Señal is a Spanish-language news service for Latin America, operated by Moonforge Arcade LLC. Every day at 8 AM (Central Time) it publishes one short video about technology, video games or cybersecurity: what happened, the verified numbers, and what you can do about it. No hype.

How we work

  • Every story is checked against its primary source, which is linked in the post.
  • Every video is reviewed and approved by a person before it is scheduled.
  • Stock footage and images are used only under licenses that allow it, and are credited.
  • We publish only to our own accounts, through each platform's official API (see our Privacy Policy).
